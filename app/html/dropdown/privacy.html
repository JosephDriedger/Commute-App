Privacy

What Commute! stores and who can see it.

person

Your Account

Your name and email, plus any profile details you choose to add (phone number, city, province, country and main method of transportation).

route

Your Trips

The places, routes and trips you save, and your alert settings. One-off trips are deleted automatically a week after they happen.

Who Can See It

Your data is stored with Google Firebase and is only accessible to you while you're signed in to your account.
