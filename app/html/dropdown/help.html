Help

Answers to common questions about Commute!

Open Routes and select Add Route. Enter where you start, where you're going and how you travel, then save.

On the Map, set a starting point and a destination, pick how you travel, then choose Get Directions. You will get the travel time, the distance and step by step directions, and you can save the trip as a route. Transit times are not available, so transit shows the driving route instead.

Open Places from the account menu and select Add Place. Give it a name like Home or School, then pick your address from the suggestions. Saved places appear at the top of the list whenever you plan a trip on the map.

Schedule shows one week at a time. Select the plus on any day to add a trip, and tick Repeat Every Week for something you do every week, like a class or a shift. Repeating trips show up on that weekday from then on. Pick a saved route on the form and we can also tell you when to leave.

Go to Schedule and select Add Trip, or the plus on the day you want. Pick a time and destination. Past trips are cleared automatically a week after they happen.

Incidents that affect your routes appear on the Alerts page, along with a suggestion to help you stay on time. The badge in the menu shows how many you have.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and choose Trouble signing in? to get a reset link. If you're already signed in, use Password Reset.
